# Skill Title In Plain Words

## Purpose

What this skill produces and why it exists, in two or three sentences.

## Source

Where the workflow was learned: the video or post title, channel or author,
and the URL. No URL, no skill. Note the evidence type honestly (full video
inspected / captions / post text and comments only) and the audit date.

## Tools used

Each tool, plugin, or stage, with the version or setting that matters.

## Workflow

Phases in order. Each phase carries:

- **Do:** the concrete actions, with real parameters and numbers.
- **Check:** how to verify the phase worked before moving on.
- **Why:** the reason this step exists, so an agent can adapt it instead of
  cargo-culting it.

## Human method distilled

The judgment calls the human made that the steps alone do not capture:
what they looked at, what they rejected, the order they chose and why.

## Depth status

FULL (the workflow was inspected end to end), PARTIAL (some stages inferred
from comments or metadata, labeled where), or the expansion path when a
video only introduces a tool.
